# Word Finalization Steps

Open `thesis/build/Nishanth_Thesis_Professor_Reviewed_Draft.docx` in Microsoft Word and complete these steps before final PDF submission.

1. Press `Ctrl + A`, then press `F9` to update fields such as page numbers.
2. Inspect the title page, declaration, dedication, acknowledgement, abstract, contents, lists, chapters, references, and appendices.
3. Check that figures and tables are not split awkwardly across pages.
4. If the department requires an automatic Table of Contents, replace the draft contents page with Word's References > Table of Contents tool.
5. Confirm the submission month/year.
6. Save the Word file.
7. Export to PDF using File > Export > Create PDF/XPS or Save As > PDF.
8. Open the exported PDF and quickly inspect every major section.
9. Upload the PDF to Turnitin/Moodle only after the visual inspection is acceptable.

Important: Do not change the reported metric values unless they are updated from the evidence files. Keep claims cautious and evidence-backed.
